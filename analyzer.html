WhatsApp (iPhone & Android)

Open chat → Tap Contact Name → Export Chat → Select Without Media → Upload the exported .txt or .zip file.

Instagram

Instagram Settings → Your Activity → Download Your Information → Choose JSON format → Upload message_1.json.

📊
CSV / JSON

Upload any structured file with standard columns: timestamp, sender, and message.

Gmail / Email

Copy email threads containing standard From: and Date: headers or Google Takeout MBOX exports.
