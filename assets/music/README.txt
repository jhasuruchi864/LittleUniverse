==================================================
OUR LITTLE UNIVERSE 💗 - BACKGROUND MUSIC GUIDE
==================================================

To play your own favorite romantic track:
1. Place your MP3 audio file in this folder: `assets/music/`
2. Name it `background.mp3`
3. Reload or open `index.html` in your web browser.

Default Audio Experience:
The website includes an integrated, dreamy Web Audio API Music Box synthesizer that plays a soothing, peaceful romantic melody automatically if `background.mp3` is absent or still loading, ensuring a delightful zero-setup romantic experience out of the box!
